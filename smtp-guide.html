SMTP Setup Guide

Connect your own email so invoice reminders are sent from your mailbox.

What is SMTP?

SMTP is the standard outgoing-mail protocol. Once you save your mailbox details in the Personal Sender Email section, the tool sends overdue-invoice reminders directly from your own address, in your name.

SMTP server The outgoing mail host given by your email provider.
Port 465 = SSL/TLS; 587 = STARTTLS. Must match the encryption below.
SMTP username Almost always your full email address, not just the part before @.
SMTP password Mailbox password, or an App Password when the provider requires one.
