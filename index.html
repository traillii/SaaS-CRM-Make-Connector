FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does the Notion synchronization work? ▼

Every client record, subscription status, and expense receipt you submit in Nordic CRM is written directly to your structured Notion databases in real time—including compressed receipt images archived cleanly inside page blocks.

Which Nordic VAT rates and currencies are supported? ▼

Our AI receipt scanner automatically detects 25% (standard), 12% (food/hospitality), 6% (transport/books), and 0% (exempt/reverse-charge) VAT rates. If your receipt is in SEK, NOK, DKK, or USD, it automatically converts the total to EUR using live exchange rates.

What happens after my 30-day free trial ends? ▼

Your existing data in Notion remains completely intact. You can choose to upgrade to Basic or Premium inside your Account & Subscription dashboard whenever you need to add more clients.
